# Enterprise Cloud Holdings: Q3 Fiscal Operating Results & Financial Review
<!-- page: 1 -->

## Consolidated Highlights & Executive Summary
Enterprise Cloud Holdings reported third-quarter revenue of **$14.82 billion**, representing a **24.6% year-over-year expansion** compared to $11.89 billion in Q3 of the prior fiscal year. Growth was propelled by accelerated enterprise adoption of accelerated AI inference clusters and sovereign cloud infrastructure across North America and EMEA.

Operating income reached **$4.80 billion**, delivering a GAAP operating margin of **32.4%**, up 180 basis points sequentially from 30.6% in Q2. Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA expanded to **$5.12 billion**, driven by automated datacenter power orchestration and sustained server utilization rates exceeding 88%.

- **Storage Margins:** Storage and Data Lake services maintained industry-leading gross margins of 72.1% due to tiered compression algorithms and NVMe-over-Fabrics deployment.

## Capital Expenditures & Datacenter Expansion (CAPEX)
Capital expenditures (CAPEX) for the quarter totaled **$3.41 billion**, an increase of **42.1% year-over-year** from $2.40 billion. The surge reflects substantial investments in next-generation GPU infrastructure, specifically the procurement of 48,000 NVIDIA H100 and B200 Tensor Core accelerators and high-bandwidth optical interconnects.

- **Datacenter Footprint:** Commissioned two tier-IV datacenter campuses in Ashburn, Virginia and Dublin, Ireland, adding 120 megawatts (MW) of critical IT load capacity.
- **Power Efficiency (PUE):** Average Datacenter Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) improved to **1.14**, beating our corporate target of 1.18 through liquid immersion cooling.
- **Cash & Liquidity:** Closed the quarter with **$8.65 billion** in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities, providing substantial runway for ongoing hyperscale datacenter expansion.
