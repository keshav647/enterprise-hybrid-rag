# FDA Phase III Clinical Trial Protocol: NCT-09482110 (SYN-402 Immunotherapy)
<!-- page: 1 -->

## 1. Study Design & Primary Efficacy Endpoints
Protocol **NCT-09482110** is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multi-center Phase III clinical trial evaluating **SYN-402** (a bispecific PD-1/TIGIT monoclonal antibody) combined with platinum-doublet chemotherapy in first-line metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) across **680 enrolled patients**.

### Cohort Stratification & Progression-Free Survival (PFS) Results
Patients were stratified by PD-L1 Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) and randomized 1:1 into **Cohort A (SYN-402 + Pemetrexed/Carboplatin)** versus **Cohort B (Standard of Care Pembrolizumab + Chemotherapy)**:

| Clinical Endpoint | Cohort A: SYN-402 Arm (n=340) | Cohort B: Control Arm (n=340) | Hazard Ratio (95% CI) | p-Value Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) | **14.8 months** | **8.2 months** | **HR = 0.54 (0.44 - 0.66)** | **p < 0.0001** |
| 12-Month Overall Survival (OS) Rate | **78.4%** | **61.2%** | **HR = 0.62 (0.50 - 0.77)** | **p = 0.0003** |
| Objective Response Rate (ORR, RECIST 1.1) | **64.5%** | **43.8%** | — | **p < 0.0001** |
| PD-L1 TPS >= 50% Subgroup Median PFS | **19.2 months** | **10.4 months** | **HR = 0.47 (0.35 - 0.63)** | **p < 0.0001** |

## 2. Patient Eligibility: Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria

### Mandatory Inclusion Criteria
1. Histologically confirmed Stage IV non-squamous or squamous NSCLC without actionable *EGFR*, *ALK*, or *ROS1* genomic alterations.
2. Measurable disease per **RECIST v1.1** assessed by Blinded Independent Central Review (BICR).
3. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of **0 or 1** and adequate hematologic/hepatic organ function (`ANC >= 1,500/mcL`, `Total Bilirubin <= 1.5x ULN`).

### Absolute Exclusion Criteria
- Prior systemic exposure to any **anti-PD-1, anti-PD-L1, anti-CTLA-4, or anti-TIGIT checkpoint inhibitor therapy** in the metastatic setting.
- Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic immunosuppression (`>10 mg/day` prednisone equivalent) within 14 days prior to randomization.
- Untreated symptomatic central nervous system (CNS) metastases or carcinomatous meningitis.
- History of non-infectious interstitial lung disease (ILD) or Grade >= 2 pneumonitis.

## 3. Dosing Regimen & CTCAE v5.0 Adverse Event Modification Rules
**Standard Dosing Schedule:** SYN-402 is administered intravenously at **1,200 mg fixed dose every 21 days (Q3W)** over a 60-minute infusion until confirmed radiographic progression or unacceptable toxicity (up to 35 cycles).

### Immune-Mediated Toxicity Management (CTCAE v5.0)
- **Grade 2 Immune-Mediated Pneumonitis or Colitis:** Immediately **withhold SYN-402** and initiate oral methylprednisolone at `1.0 to 2.0 mg/kg/day`. Resume SYN-402 only after toxicity resolves to **Grade <= 1** and corticosteroid taper reaches `<= 10 mg/day` prednisone equivalent.
- **Grade 3 or Grade 4 Pneumonitis, Hepatitis (`AST/ALT > 8x ULN`), or Myocarditis:** **Permanently discontinue SYN-402**, hospitalize patient immediately, and administer intravenous high-dose methylprednisolone (`2.0 to 4.0 mg/kg/day`), adding infliximab (`5 mg/kg`) or mycophenolate mofetil if no clinical improvement occurs within **48 hours**.
